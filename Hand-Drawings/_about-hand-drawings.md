## Transcription — the "Burning Book / Heart Fire" face drawing

*An ink drawing of a head/effigy wrapped in straps, ringed with hand-lettered labels and a manifesto. Partial transcription; `[?]` marks uncertain readings.*

**Manifesto (upper right):**
> "I will Burn my Fire Every Day to Show that my Heart Fire will Send a Wave / Mass Exodus for This Place and Beyond to the Outer lands of our Domestic Reality."
>
> "I will Burn… This Truest Fire All Mighty And Inter[-]Local Of All [?]"

**Labels around the face:** "THINKING" · "FLAMES" · "FEELING [?]"

**Left-side verse (partial):**
> "I will [be] her[e] / Don't be ashamed / I am good 3 [on?] my own… look the Spark of Others… I am a ball of energy… place the Spark of the others… I burn the brightest… I am the Spark… the one would burn 30 strong / Become one in the fire's / I'm the last to become a duty / Person [?]… We will Become One."

**Bottom annotations:** "Multi-Directional Heartfelt Beats" · "Don't Be Scared" · "Where were you?" · **"explore this drawing"**

*Annotation: This is the conceptual seed of *The Burning Book of Brian* — "my Heart Fire will Send a Wave," "Multi-Directional Heartfelt Beats" (the pulsing LPG heart), and the communal "We will Become One" (the royal we). It doubles as a personal mantra ("I will Burn my Fire Every Day"). Prime voice + values material.*
