# Uncle Charlie — Poetry & Words

Charlie's old site (howhowhow.com) has a hidden **"Words"** section — a set of typewriter-style poems/song-lyrics, each stored as a scanned **GIF image** behind broken JavaScript pop-up links. They're a prime window into his **poetic voice**: lowercase, free-verse, surreal list-poems, dark/playful humor, consumer-culture satire, and recurring **fire / chicken / wolf** imagery (the chicken/rooster obsession that runs straight into *Mr. Nice Guy*, *Hot Mama*, and the *Red Hot Cock*).

**Source:** http://www.howhowhow.com/words.html → individual poems at `howhowhow.com/poems/<name>.gif`.
**Status:** 3 of 17 transcribed below; the rest are catalogued with URLs (they're images, so each needs a quick visual transcription — easy to finish on request).

*Transcriptions are verbatim, including his spellings/typos (part of the voice). Last updated: June 21, 2026.*

## Transcribed poems

### "new world order" — a fake "top 40 hit" / anti-consumerist song
> this is my latest idea for the new world order
> please add it to you cache..
>
> we are going to refine this and make stickers available..
> $$$$$$$$$$$ will be had
>
> hey dad / what in the bag dad / you got some oil there / its in your hair there
> hey dad / say what's your bag dad / its in the air there / what to care dare..
> hey dad / i got your bag dad / its mine for sure there / but there's no cure there
> hey dad / what's this bag dad / its not for sure dad / its kinda sad dad
>
> you love it / you leave it, you leave it alone -- yeah (×3)
>
> Say damn dad / you got your bag dad / you got every thing / you make the earth sting
> Say damn dad / there goes the bag dad / you took every thing / you make the heart sting
> Say damn dad / you dropped the bag dad / you smoked all the toy's / you took all the boy's
>
> say damn / bag dad / drop dad / drop dad / drop dad / drop dad……….fades out
>
> this is my latest top 40 hit.. enjoy

*Annotation: ecological/consumer lament ("you make the earth sting") disguised as a bubblegum pop song — satire + sincerity at once, classic Charlie. Note the wink-wink "$$$ will be had / make stickers available" jab at commercialization.*

### "so here it is" — a birthday list-poem
> The quaking of new / taking freash the views / long day's short nights / forgotten fouls / rotisery chicken / collar green / shirt stains / barbie in heat / dancing cats / sleeping dog / mashed potatoes / malto meal? / all dressed up / prom night / drive in / relentless chanting / sore body / strong minds / cheerfilled bliss / a quiet kiss / burning piss? / before its gone / get it now / half off / two for one / one for all / candy bar gigglesworth! / chuckles the clown / all over town / all ways moving around. / so on this day / no dismay / havin a happy birthday!!! / happy birthday polly!

*Annotation: a tumbling free-association list (Americana + carnival imagery — "chuckles the clown," "candy bar gigglesworth!") that resolves into a warm birthday wish. The "two for one / one for all" cadence and exclamation bursts are textbook Charlie.*

### "walking with chickens" — closest thing to an ars poetica
> its a blur as time shifts the parallel / worlds collide no reality.. dreams alone / sensless attempts to hold / notions of dis-belief / a successful failure / one might add? / crawling home / tail straight and true / fire candy / sour fruit / dirty nickles / priceless bill / no one left / only rights / lone lovers / give / as trembling hands feel water / and yet the fire lives inside / the relentless wolf / poor chickens / cluck

*Annotation: "a successful failure / one might add?" is a perfect self-portrait of his ethos — and "the fire lives inside / the relentless wolf / poor chickens / cluck" fuses his fire and chicken/predator motifs into one image. Great pull-quote material.*
