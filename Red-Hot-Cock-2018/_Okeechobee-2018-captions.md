# Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival 2018 (#OMF18) — Red Hot Cock / ASS Camp

Photos by **Kris Krüg**. The year **Kris camped with Charlie** and the crew built/showed the **jackal-headed rooster** — "Uncle Charlie's Red Hot Cock" / "The Waking Bird Dog," the flaming "cock spitter" (it shoots fire from its beak and rocks). This was its **Okeechobee, FL showing in March 2018** (the piece was also Charlie's Burning Man 2018 honorarium later that year).

- **Event:** Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival 2018 (#OMF18), Okeechobee FL · taken ~March 1, 2018
- **Artwork:** `red-hot-cock` (the jackal-rooster flame sculpture)

## Crew key (who's who) — ASS Camp at Okeechobee 2018
- **Charlie "Blackcat" Smith** — cap, mirrored sunglasses, vest, chest/arm tattoos — including a **tattoo of the rooster sculpture itself on his stomach**.
- **Paperboy** — straw/pith hat + clear glasses; key builder (drills/attaches the rooster's metal "feather" scales; fixes the propane "poofers").
- **Little Ed** — younger, black tank top; raised the hand-lettered sign-flag.
- **Lil Joe** — crew (seen "faded," late night). *(Likely the "Little Joe" name in the 2023 interview transcript — so both a Lil Joe and a Little Ed are in the crew.)*
- **Jared Chen** — crew, nicknamed "resident alien."
- **Dana Chambers** — crew.
- **Prescott McCarthy** — crew.
- **Kris Krüg** — photographer/quartermaster (behind the camera; "the whole crew minus me").
