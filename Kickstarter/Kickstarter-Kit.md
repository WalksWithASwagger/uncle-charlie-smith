## 0. Campaign at a glance
- **Platform:** Kickstarter (all-or-nothing; miss the goal = $0 and no fees → **set an achievable goal**). ~8–10% total fees. ~14-day payout after success.
- **Goal logic:** total project budget is larger than the Kickstarter ask. **Crowdfund a clear slice** (e.g., materials + transport) and cover the rest with grants/commissions/in-kind (see `Funding/Funder-Tracker.md`). A *reachable* goal that funds fast builds momentum; over-ambitious all-or-nothing goals fail.
- **Suggested ask:** **$18–22K** (the "build + get-it-to-the-playa" slice), framed against a transparent full budget.
- **Window:** **21–30 days** (shorter converts better). Launch to a warm list day one.
- **Timing vs. BM:** ideally **launch by mid-July**, close before the August build crunch. (OMG ticket sale is July 29; Franconia/grant deadlines run parallel — see trackers.)
- **Add tax-deductible path:** route larger gifts through a **fiscal sponsor** (Fractured Atlas) so donors can deduct — mention it for the high tiers.

## 1. Campaign page — narrative (draft)

### Title
**The Burning Book of Brian — a memorial fire for the man who wrote the book on Burning Man**

### Subtitle / blurb
*Charlie "Uncle Charlie" Smith is building a 15-foot steel book with a human heart that beats fire — a one-time burn for our friend Brian Doherty. Help us feed the fire.*

### The story (body)
**Brian Doherty literally wrote the book on Burning Man** — *This Is Burning Man*. This spring, we lost him. Charlie met Brian at HEARTH in 2000, the very first fire sculpture he built at the Heart of Burning Man, and they've been friends across 26 years of dust and flame ever since.

So Charlie's building him a goodbye worthy of it.

*The Burning Book of Brian* is a **15-foot steel-and-fire effigy of an open book**, lying back toward the sky, with a **giant human heart piercing its core** — an LPG flame effect that pulses, *thump-THUMP-thump*, like a heartbeat for the whole playa to gather around. Its pages are being **hand-skinned by 8–10 artists** with words and symbols for Brian. On Wednesday night, we'll process out from the Cacophony Society Cocktail Hour and **burn it once** — solemn and joyful both, because Brian would've wanted it both ways.

**This is how Charlie has built for 30 years:** not *for* a community but *with* one. He's the "Johnny Appleseed of Burning Man" — he travels, teaches people to weld, builds the thing together, and gives it away. Burning Man honorarium artist a dozen times over; the mind behind Uncle Charlie's Red Hot Cock, the Fleeble Flobbler, the 24/7 Time Star. *(He came back to build this after losing most of the use of his left arm — which is exactly why the crew, and your help, matters.)*

This year the Burning Book is **not a funded Burning Man honorarium** — it's ours to make happen. Every dollar buys steel, propane, and the truck that gets it to the desert. Every log we burn carries a name.

**Feed the fire with us.** 🔥

- **When do I get my reward?** Prints/patches/stickers ship after the event (~Oct–Nov 2026); experiences are scheduled with you.

## 6. Risks & challenges (Kickstarter requires this — and it's a selling point)
Charlie has built and safely operated large fire sculptures at Burning Man for ~25 years. The main risks are **weather/logistics** (desert build) and **fire-permitting timelines** — both of which he plans for rigorously: redundant fire suppression, an on-site FAST-approved safety crew, a full Leave No Trace plan, and contingency in the budget. Reward fulfillment is simple (prints/patches) and handled after the event. *If fire approvals shift, we'll adapt the format (flame-effects vs. full burn) and keep every backer informed.*

## 7. Launch & backer-update plan
- **Pre-launch (1–2 wks):** build a "notify on launch" list (Charlie's + KK's networks, ASS Camp alumni, the burner/festival community, Brian's circle/Cacophony Society). Aim to fund **30%+ in 48 hrs** from the warm list — early momentum drives the algorithm.
- **Day 1:** email + IG/FB (use the in-voice captions in `Charlie-Content-Drafts.md`), the video, and the Red Hot Cock hero shots.
- **Mid-campaign:** a build-log update (welding the heart), a "name a page" push, a stretch-goal idea (e.g., a documented build film, or gifting a cauldron post-event).
- **Updates cadence:** post every 3–5 days (progress, crew stories, Brian tributes). Backers fund people they trust.
- **After funding:** monthly build updates → the burn → fulfillment. Turn backers into Patreon/contraptionism.com members for *sustained* support.
