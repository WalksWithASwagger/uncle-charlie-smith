## Canon confirmed / corrected (fed into `works.json` → `triple-bypass`)

1. **The name.** It started as the **"Quad Pass" (Quadrapass)** and was renamed **"Triple Bypass"** *mid-build* — a heart-attack joke ("it's nearly given us all a heart attack"). Previously the archive listed Quadrapass as a vague "related" work; it's the same piece, renamed.
2. **The lead time.** "We've been working for over a year and a half to get here" — not just the "6 weeks in studio" the 2023 interview implied.
3. **The road to it.** Charlie & **Jamie Ladet** toured the US building gift-economy fire cauldrons *en route* to South Africa.
4. **The symbol system was crowd-sourced.** Workshop participants designed the glyphs skinning the sculpture — dragon-on-anvil (the blacksmith can't exist without fire), circle-in-square (infinity), lightning (energy) — chosen for personal resonance, meant to be legible *while burning*.
5. **First AfrikaBurn logistics were brutal** — couldn't burn in December; artist tickets fell through; a property was pulled; airlines went bankrupt; flooding forced a date change. Charlie reframes every setback as a gift ("worked in our favor").

## Quotable moments (verbatim, lightly cleaned)

> "Welcome to the Quad Pass project — which now has been renamed 'Triple Bypass.' It's nearly given us all a heart attack."

> **"Thematically here with Africa Burns there are no spectators — so what we have is full-on collaboration, everybody getting involved on this together."**

> "The symbolism is *you*. We're asking *you*. It's on you all."

> "We aren't telling people what to put on; we're saying put something on that is resonant, something that has meaning to you. We'll be able to read this thing while it's on fire — everybody's intent will show through."

> "I love people; I want to share. That's why we're here, that's why we work so hard to be here."

> "The passageways of the fire are the Earth blood running through the sculpture."

> "We're not really familiar with the metric system, so we made a little miscalculation — it's about twice as big as we would normally order." *(the human, funny one)*

> "They'll say: okay, we can get together and make something — *so what else are we going to make together?*"
