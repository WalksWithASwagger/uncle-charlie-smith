**[On the community method]**
CS: The way I build artwork utilizes community — we bring the art into the community to be constructed from local resources. I do community workshops where people of all skill levels come to put effort into expanding their minds and relationships, and take away an experience where they become part of the artwork forever. As long as the piece exists, their energy is in it. They learn how to weld and fabricate, and they learn about each other.

One of the largest exercises was in 2005 — the Synapsis project, done with Jaime [Ladet] (we're not together anymore, but we were then) and my children. We created six cities, six sculptures over a six-month run — builds all over the country — leaving the sculptures in those communities to use and take care of. They were built, then brought together [at Burning Man], and they all met each other in that circle of fire cauldrons, and they all got to tell the story, because the communication was in a symbol system they created themselves. The only words were the expression between each other. Wrapping that whole country up into one bundle created a huge amount of relationships and connection through the community of art — and that's still resonating today, because a high percentage of people who took part have accelerated into their own art and their own teams.
