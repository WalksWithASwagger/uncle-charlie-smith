## Midjourney style cues (from his actual aesthetic)
Use these to steer prompts — drawn from the brand/worldview docs:
- **Materials & finish:** raw/rusted mild steel + stainless, welded plate "feather" scales, bolted armatures, patina, sparks, "Charlie patchwork" of copper/brass/glass/steel.
- **Forms:** giant mythic animal/deity effigies — jackal-headed rooster, elephant Ganesha, rotund clown, seven-pointed star, fertility caldrons; kinetic teeter-totters and rideable bases.
- **Fire:** wood-fired cauldrons + LPG flame "poofers"; flame jets from beaks/hearts; glowing-red-hot metal at night.
- **Setting & mood:** dusty playa / festival field, night with string lights and crowds, warm sunrise silhouettes, communal/ritual energy, "transformational fire," DIY maximalist.
- **Sample seed:** *"towering rusted-steel jackal-headed rooster fire sculpture, welded plate feathers, spitting a tall flame from its beak at night, festival crowd rocking it on a teeter base, string lights, dust, glowing embers, cinematic --ar 3:2"*
