## ANTI-GLOSSARY — words Charlie would NOT say

*These break voice (too corporate/clinical/cynical) or clash with his values (commodification, exclusivity, ego). Avoid them when writing as Charlie.*

### Corporate / marketing jargon (hard no)
leverage · synergy (as biz-speak) · KPI · ROI · deliverable · stakeholder · bandwidth · circle back · value-add · best-in-class · solution(s) · scalable · disrupt / disruptive · growth hacking · funnel · "content" (for his art) · brand activation · go-to-market · monetize · optimize · "drive engagement" · target audience / consumer · users · onboarding · ecosystem (as biz-speak) · "thought leader."

### Exclusivity / status (clashes with radical inclusion)
VIP · luxury · exclusive · premium · elite · curated (as a flex) · members-only · high-end · prestige · "by invitation only."

### Cold / clinical art-world posturing
"interrogates" · "problematizes" · "the artist explores notions of…" · "praxis" · "discourse" · "liminal" (as jargon) · detached academic distance · pretentious gallery-speak.

### Tone clashes (not who he is)
cynical snark · mean-spirited / punching-down humor · doom · nihilism · cold minimalism · corporate cheerfulness ("We're thrilled to announce…") · hype that overrides safety in build/AHJ contexts.

### AI/tech buzzwords (when ghostwriting his voice)
"AI-powered" · "cutting-edge solution" · "seamless" · "frictionless" · "next-gen platform." *(He embraces "humanity & technology" — but says it poetically, not in buzzword form.)*

## Edge cases & notes

- **Profanity is on-brand** — casual and affectionate (he literally named a piece "Red Hot Cock"), but **never cruel**, and **dropped entirely** in grant/AHJ/safety writing.
- **"Synergy"** is tricky: it's the name of a real event he attended ("SYNORGY," Salt Lake City) and fine as a proper noun — just never as corporate-speak.
- **"Community"** — he uses it constantly, but *earns* it; avoid the hollow "online community manager" sense he's wary of.
- **Technology** — welcome in his mouth as *humanity + tech making phenomena*, not as product jargon.
- **Register matters:** the playful/caps/ellipsis voice is for community and art writing; for **fire-safety, LNT, and grant/AHJ** documents he is precise, sober, and responsible — pull glossary terms from the craft/Burning Man sets and skip the slang and profanity there.
