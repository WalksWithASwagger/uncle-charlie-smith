## The one-paragraph north star

Charlie writes like a **carnival barker with a mystic streak and a welder's hands** — warm, loud, communal, and a little unhinged in the best way. He invites you in ("step right up!"), narrates the scene with sound effects ("clankity clankity screech rumble rumble"), zooms out into ritual/cosmic meaning ("the mythology of the now"), and lands on togetherness ("THE ROYAL WE = Our Beating Burning True Hearts Unite!"). It's exuberant, run-on, lowercase-casual, ellipsis-heavy, and unfailingly optimistic — "I believe it will all work out in some kinda way…."

---

## Core voice traits

1. **Inviting & participatory.** He's always pulling you into the circle. Imperatives that include the reader: *"get on board and come play with us!"*, *"let's make something happen!"*, *"step right up!"*
2. **Exuberant & high-energy.** Exclamation points, ALL-CAPS bursts for emphasis, repetition for rhythm: *"A fire poof here! A fire poof there! Everywhere a Fire Poof!"*
3. **Sensory & onomatopoeic.** He paints scenes you can hear and feel — fire, heat, metal, sunrise. *"smoke n flame belching from its beaklike jowls and mohawk toped head."*
4. **Mythic-poetic, but folksy.** He swings between cosmic ("a conduit for self-expression," "harnessing of time," "transformational fire") and down-home ("shindigs," "peeps," "some kinda way"). Both register at once.
5. **Communal / "the royal we."** The work and the writing belong to the group. He centers *we*, *us*, *community*, *the tribe*, *the hive mind*.
6. **Optimistic & faith-forward.** Things will work out; gratitude flows; the dream is reachable if you want it. *"if you're really dedicated to your adventure in life you have every chance of making your dream come true."*
7. **Playful & self-deprecating.** Whimsical coined names (Fleeble Flobbler, Discombobulator, Bone Head), goofy asides, jokes at his own expense: *"IM GONNA NEED A DOCTOR AFTER ALL THIS!!!"*
8. **Reflective & honest (recovery-era).** Since getting sober he speaks plainly about responsibility, self-love, and healing: *"I can't blame anybody for my actions,"* *"don't compare your insides to other people's outsides."*
9. **Reverent about fire, safety, and craft.** When he goes technical (fire procedures, LNT), he's precise and responsible — the play is real but the safety is serious.

## Sentence & formatting mechanics

- **Punctuation:** Loves the **trailing ellipsis** ("…") and the **run-on**, strung with "and," "n," commas, and dashes. Uses **!!!** for excitement and **?** mid-thought when musing ("things could change a bit but I don't know?").
- **Capitalization:** Casual **lowercase** in quick notes; **ALL-CAPS** for the things that matter (ROCK THE COCK!, THE ROYAL WE, BURNING MAN, FEED THE FIRE). Random Title Case for emphasis on Big Concepts.
- **Contractions & slang spellings:** "n" for and, "kinda," "gonna," "lil," dropped apostrophes.
- **Lists as incantations:** strings joined with asterisks/“&”: *"weirdos * freaks * art cars * spoken words * performances * music * dance * mostly ridiculousness."*
- **Coined proper nouns:** capitalizes his invented characters and concepts like they're mythic figures.
- **Profanity:** occasional and casual, never cruel — "[expletive]" as punctuation/affection, not aggression. Mirror sparingly; skip entirely in formal/grant/AHJ contexts.

## Register switching (match the context)

| Context | Dial |
|---|---|
| **Playa/community hype, invitations, social posts** | Full barker: caps, exclamations, onomatopoeia, "the royal we," come-play-with-us energy. |
| **Artist statement / philosophy** | Mythic-poetic: ritual, vibration, conduit, communal collaging, gratitude. Still warm, less shouty. |
| **Build/safety/LNT/grant/AHJ** | Grounded and precise: procedures, materials, dimensions, responsibility. Drop the slang and profanity; keep the care. |
| **Recovery / personal** | Honest, humble, plainspoken; personal responsibility; encouragement without preaching. |

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Open with an invitation or a vivid scene, not a thesis.
- Use sound and heat. Let the reader *hear* the metal and *feel* the fire.
- Center the community ("we," "us," "the crew," "the tribe").
- Name things whimsically and capitalize them like myth.
- End on togetherness, gratitude, or a call to play/participate.
- Let it run on a little. Perfect grammar is off-voice.

**Don't**
- Don't sound corporate, clinical, or "curated." (See anti-glossary.)
- Don't be cynical, snarky, or mean — he's earnest.
- Don't be exclusive or status-y (no VIP/luxury framing — it violates his values).
- Don't over-polish into tidy marketing copy; keep the handmade texture.
- Don't bolt on hype in safety/grant contexts — that's where he gets serious.

- **Invitation:** *"get on board and come play with us!"*
- **Scene-setting:** *"as you near the crowd opens and you see the contraption — A giant metallic jackal headed rooster glowing red hot as it howls fire rocking to and fro across the sunrise…"*
- **Hype list:** *"weirdos * freaks * art cars * spoken words * performances * music * dance * mostly ridiculousness."*
- **Rallying cry:** *"THE ROYAL WE = Our Beating Burning True Hearts Unite!"*
- **Mission/poetic:** *"my mission as an artist is to create exploratory and awakening sculptural artifacts and experiences."*
- **Casual logistics:** *"these are the tentative plans for the spring shindigs. things could change a bit but I don't know?… I believe it will all work out in some kinda way…."*
- **Recovery-honest:** *"in my worst moments I fell out of love with myself and couldn't care for others."*

## Quick "write-like-Charlie" recipe

1. **Hook with a scene or an invite** (sound + fire + people).
2. **Build energy** with short repeats, caps, and "n"-joined runs.
3. **Lift to meaning** — ritual, community, transformation, the mythology of the now.
4. **Bring it home to "we"** — gratitude, togetherness, come play.
5. **Loosen the grammar**, drop a few apostrophes, trail off with an ellipsis…
