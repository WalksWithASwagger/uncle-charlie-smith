## 2. National grants / fellowships
**Emergency / medical (injury-relevant, rolling, realistic):**
- **Rauschenberg Medical** — up to $5K; recent medical costs; AGI ≤ $80K indiv; apply within 6 mo. nyfa.org/awards-grants/rauschenberg-medical-emergency-grants
- **CERF+ Emergency Relief** — $3K; best metal/craft match; + **Get Ready** up to $1K for studio fire-suppression. cerfplus.org/grants
- **Gottlieb Emergency** — up to $15K (typ. $5K); sculptors named; "mature artist + catastrophe." gottliebfoundation.org/emergency-grant
- **FCA Emergency** — $500–$3K, **only if a dated exhibition is jeopardized** (not general medical).

**Career / project (self-apply):**
- **Pollock-Krasner** — $5K–$30K unrestricted, **need-based**, rolling, no nomination/degree. pkf.org/apply
- **Artadia Atlanta** — $15K unrestricted, **open call Sep 1–Oct 1, 2026**, 2-yr Atlanta-area residency. artadia.org/award-city/atlanta
- **Harpo Foundation** — up to $10K for "under-recognized" artists; ~April deadline. harpofoundation.org
- **Creative Capital** — $15K–$50K projects (also State of the Art Prize $10K/state — better odds). creative-capital.org

**Nomination-only (cultivate nominators):** United States Artists ($50K) · Joan Mitchell Fellowship ($60K/5yr) · FCA Grants to Artists ($45K).
**NEA:** no direct individual visual-art grants — access only indirectly via NEA-funded orgs / GA Council / South Arts regrants.

## 7. Crowdfunding / membership (2026)
- **Kickstarter** — best for the one-time build; ~8–10% total fees; **all-or-nothing** (miss goal = $0, no fees); ~14-day payout. *(See the Kickstarter kit.)*
- **Patreon** — recurring; ~10% + processing; best once there's an audience → tie to contraptionism.com.
- **Patronicity ("crowdgranting")** — crowdfunding **+ matching grant**; check for an active Georgia/Atlanta match partner — potentially big for a community-sited piece.
- **ioby** — hyper-local community crowd-resourcing (good for an Atlanta public install). **Ko-fi** — 0% tip jar.
- **Pattern for big builds:** bundle → commission/honorarium + fiscal-sponsored deductible donations + Kickstarter or matching-grant.
