# People

## Charlie "Blackcat" Smith
The artist. Atlanta-based sculptor, fire artist, flame-effects operator, metal fabricator, art director, and community-workshop producer; owner of **Sparseland Studios / Sparceland LLC**. Persona **"Uncle Charlie"** (front-and-center in [[works/red-hot-cock]] and ASS Camp). "Johnny Appleseed of Burning Man." Multi-time Burning Man Honorarium artist (2000→2018). See `Charlie-BlackCat-Smith-Knowledge-Base.md` for the full bio.
- **CV highlights:** BFA, Atlanta College of Art (**1988–1992**) · internship→employment with mixed-media sculptor [[#Carl André Davidt]] (Andre Studios, 1991–94) · film industry — set production & design, design-house specialty product dev, crew lead / AD assistant (1994–97) · founded Sparceland Studios (1997→) · Tennessee flame special-effects licensing (2006–2022) · community-built sculpture workshops since 2000 (SF, Seattle, Austin, Denver, NYC, Atlanta). Self-described "dream producer." Full canonical CV: [[one-pager-charlie-approved]].
- **~2017 injury:** lost most use of his left arm (~40% remains) + back problems; gave back his 2017 grant; came back for the 2018 honorarium ([[works/red-hot-cock]]).
- **Links:** sparceland.com · IG @artofsuchnsuch · facebook.com/charlieblackcatsmith · facebook.com/sparselandstudios · PayPal.Me/thewakingbird

## Kris Krüg (KK)
Photographer / creative technologist. Met Charlie at **Okeechobee Music Festival** via [[#Tyler Hansen]] while working with [[organizations#KultureHaus]], bonding over [[works/fleeble-flobbler]]. Joined [[organizations#ASS Camp]] as **quartermaster** for [[works/red-hot-cock]] (2018), documenting the build in photo/video (the bulk of this archive's `rights=kk-owned` images). Wrote the 2023 "Johnny Appleseed of Burning Man" profile + the Red Hot Cock/Shrine story. Shares a sobriety journey with Charlie. Ongoing collaborator.

## Carl André Davidt
Atlanta mixed-media sculptor; proprietor of **Andre Studios**. Charlie's first professional mentor — a college internship that turned into employment, **1991–94** (his 2023 CV abbreviates this to 1991–93; end-year to confirm). The fabrication-and-leadership grounding that, with his later film-industry years, fed the founding of Sparceland Studios in 1997.
