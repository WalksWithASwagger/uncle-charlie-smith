## Bio

Charlie "Blackcat" Smith is a true-hearted human being who has been obsessed with art and mechanism and how it all works since childhood. He's lived on both coasts, and the North, South and Midwest, and considers his early years as an American nomad. He's now a visual artist, sculptor, flame effects operator, metal fabricator, and dream producer based in Atlanta, GA, where he earned his BFA from the Atlanta College of Art from 1988–1992.

Following an internship followed by employment with mixed media sculptor Carl André Davidt (Andre Studios) 1991–1994, Charlie dove into the film industry, notably in the realms of set production and design house specialty product development. There he built high-end fabrication and leadership skills as a crew lead and assistant to art directors, developing his now well-known talent for marinating highly creative and collaborative crews, fostering them in bringing out their best work.

Sparseland Studios emerged from the alchemy of these experiences, skills, and developed connections. Since its inception in 1997, countless projects have been conceived and manifested at this SW Atlanta art studio — sculptural, high-end design-build, architectural, art furniture, fine arts, and more. Sparseland has also enabled Charlie to enrich thousands through singular visions, enabling the growth of visiting artists and developing collaborative projects, which continue flowing through his studio today.
