- **Dimensions:** —
- **Motifs:** [[motifs#global-art|global-art]], [[motifs#instigating-afrikaburn|instigating-afrikaburn]]

Fire cauldron begun as the "Quad Pass" (Quadrapass) and renamed "Triple Bypass" mid-build (a heart-attack joke). The first Burning-Man-represented artifact at the inaugural AfrikaBurn ("Africa Burns", 2007, at the Bijou in Observatory, Cape Town); a ~year-and-a-half effort. Charlie & Jamie Ladet toured the US building gift-economy fire cauldrons en route; workshop participants designed the sculpture's own symbol system (dragon-on-anvil, circle-in-square/infinity, lightning).
