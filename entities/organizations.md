# Organizations & Networks

## Sparseland Studios / Sparceland LLC
Charlie's Atlanta studio and business entity (since 1997). Public hub: **sparceland.com** (alias artofsuchnsuch.com). Workshops for the 2026 [[works/burning-book]] community build run here. Studio: 680 Murphy Ave SW, Unit 6026, Atlanta, GA 30310.

## ASS Camp
**A.S.S. = Art Support Services** (KK's expansion) / **Art of Such & Such** (Charlie's). The "ragtag band of merry misfits" theme camp behind [[works/red-hot-cock]]. KK joined as quartermaster. See [[people#ASS Camp crew (Red Hot Cock, 2018)]].

## Burning Man Project
The org behind Black Rock City. Charlie is a multi-time **Honorarium** artist (2000→2018: [[works/hearth]], [[works/synapses-tour]], [[works/contraptions]], [[works/fire-birds]], [[works/big-bully]], [[works/infinite-infant]], [[works/time-star]], [[works/red-hot-cock]]). Photo archive at gallery.burningman.org + history.burningman.org. Note: the 2026 [[works/burning-book]] is **presented as a memorial project but is NOT a verified 2026 honorarium** (not on the 75-project list).

## Black Rock Arts Foundation (BRAF)
Seed-grant source. Funded [[works/tunnel-of-transformation]] (2003) and supported [[works/time-star]]'s Atlanta install.

## Art on the BeltLine
Atlanta public-art program that hosted [[works/time-star]] for ~2.5 years at the Historic Fourth Ward (2015–2018).

## KultureHaus
The camp/crew KK was working with at Okeechobee when he met Charlie.

## Love Burn
Miami regional burn; [[works/hot-mama]] and [[works/mr-nice-guy]] shown there as recently as 2015.

## Suwannee Hulaween / Spirit Lake
Florida festival where [[works/fleeble-flobbler]] appeared as a flame-effects machine (2023).
