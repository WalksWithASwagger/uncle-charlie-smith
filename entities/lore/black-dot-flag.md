# The Black-Dot Flag — "The Period"

*A hidden story, in Charlie's own words (told to Kris, 2026). The black dot on a white field — a recurring flag/icon across the camp and the work.*

## What it means
The flag is a **period — the dot at the end of a sentence.**

> "This IS IT! The end of a sentence. I surrender — but this is it: as in there's no contest, no winning or losing.
>
> I fly the black on white. The circle complete. The field is blank and open to any direction — the sphere, the never-ending cycle. But as an icon it bares the simplest: as in *I am, and nothing more.*"
> — Charlie "Blackcat" Smith

## Reading it
- **Period / full stop** — finality, surrender, "this is it." Not defeat — the end of the contest itself.
- **Black on white** — the simplest possible icon; an "I am, and nothing more."
- **The circle / sphere** — complete, blank, open to any direction; the never-ending cycle.

Flown across **ASS Camp / Burning Man 2018** (the Red Hot Cock build) — see the dust-storm frames in `Catalog/images.csv` (search `black-dot flag`), e.g. crew waving it by the yellow Penske truck, the two-men-hug under it in golden playa dust (`asscamp-dayzero0U6A6831`, `FB_IMG_1536857064269`).
