## Who is Charlie BlackCat Smith

Charlie Smith is an Atlanta-based sculptor who, for nearly three decades, has built giant interactive metal machines that burn — kinetic effigies, fire cauldrons, and flame-effect contraptions that only come alive when a crowd shows up to operate them. Kris Krüg has called him **"The Johnny Appleseed of Burning Man,"** and the name fits: Charlie's defining move isn't just making a sculpture, it's traveling, teaching people to weld and fabricate, building the piece *with* a community, and then often gifting that community its own fire sculpture to keep.

He describes himself as a "Visual Artist, Sculpture, Flame Effects Operator, Metal Fabricator, Art Director, Consultant, Idea man, Interactive Art Installation Designer, Community Workshop Producer." His self-styled list of hats is a good tell — he's a maker and a connector in equal measure.

**CV facts (from sparceland.com/bio-shift):** BFA, **Atlanta College of Art (1992)** · film/TV/trade-show props & special effects (**1994–97**) · **Sparceland Studios (1997→)** · **Tennessee flame special-effects licensing (2006–2022)** · Burning Man Honoraria years **2000 through 2018** · community-built sculpture workshops since 2000 across San Francisco, Seattle, Austin, Denver, New York, and Atlanta.

**On the names:**
- **"BlackCat" / "Blackcat"** — his long-running artist handle.
- **"Uncle Charlie"** — the affectionate persona, front-and-center in the 2018 piece *Uncle Charlie's Red Hot Cock* and the framing for ASS Camp.
- **"Chaznuts"** — a community nickname (used by fellow burners in the wild, e.g. Burning Man Journal comments).
- **"chunks"** — his old-school handle (his ~2007 contact email was *chunks@mindspring.com*, site *howhowhow.com*).

His **artist statement** centers on art as a release valve for the chaos of modern life — "The clogged brain needs plunging and a good flush from the chaos-filled world we live in today" — and on art as a communal, ritual, participatory act rather than an object to be passively viewed. He repeatedly cites a lifelong inspiration from his **Scandinavian heritage** and "the playful exploration of my inner child," which surface as mythical, character-driven creatures (roosters, jackals, clowns, stars).

## Contraptionism — the throughline

Charlie just registered **contraptionism.com** (as of mid-2026 it resolves but is empty/parked — no content yet). The word is the right label for a body of work that has always been about *contraptions*: machines that are absurd, mythic, mechanical, kinetic, and fire-breathing, that demand human bodies to make them go.

**This isn't a new word for him — it's a 20-year callback.** His **2007 Burning Man installation was literally titled "Contraptions"** (Aug 27–Sep 4, 2007, Black Rock City — per his howhowhow.com archive). So registering *contraptionism.com* in 2026 revives and elevates a name he coined for his own work nearly two decades ago — turning a title into an *-ism*. That's a strong signal he's consciously framing his life's work as a movement/philosophy.

The word literally appears in his own description of *Uncle Charlie's Red Hot Cock*:
> "as you near the crowd opens and you see **the contraption** — A giant metallic jackal-headed rooster glowing red hot as it howls fire rocking to and fro across the sunrise…"

**Why "contraptionism" captures 30 years of his work:**
- **Kinetic & participatory** — teeter-totters, rideable bases, hand-pumped flame "heartbeats." The art doesn't work without the crowd; the crowd's physical energy literally powers it.
- **Mechanical + mythic** — hard metallic textures and "hard lines of expressionism" fused with fairy-tale characters and fertility/heritage symbolism.
- **Fire as the social enabler** — see his Leonardo essay *The Communal Caldron of Fire*: he replaces the prototypical bonfire with a portable metal "Fire Cauldron" that becomes a "central axis point" for community.
- **Built by community, gifted to community** — workshops where participants learn to weld and fabricate, then often keep the resulting sculpture.

**The sharper frame — builder of social machines.** The object is never the whole work; the real work is the crew, the ritual, the burn procedure, the shared danger, the story, and the memory-body that forms around the machine. A working definition: *contraptionism is ritual-scale, community-built, kinetic fire sculpture where the machine exists to make people gather, move, feed, witness, and remember together.* Positioning line: **Charlie makes contraptionist fire rituals — community-built metal mythologies that turn fabrication, fuel, danger, memory, and participation into temporary social architecture.** (Full thesis + motif glossary: `entities/motifs.md`. Note: "contraptionism" is an interpretive lens, not Burning Man-official terminology.)

> **Strategic read:** The new domain looks like Charlie moving to *name and brand* this lifelong practice — turning a 30-year instinct into a stated philosophy ("Contraptionism"), and likely a hub for documenting, teaching, or selling the work. As of mid-2026 it's an empty placeholder. Note this is distinct from his 2023 "what's next" piece, the *Solar Scope* (see catalog) — so by 2026 he has at least three threads going: the Burning Book (memorial), the Solar Scope (commission), and Contraptionism (the brand/platform). **Worth asking him directly what he wants contraptionism.com to be — and whether you can help build it.**

## Artist philosophy & method

A few recurring principles run through everything he makes:

**"Communal collaging."** His own term for his collaborative technique — building ideas, symbols, and designs into a sculpture *together* with a group, so the finished object holds the community's shared dreams. He frames this as "a gateway for these expressions and explorations of personal and shared dreams."

**Art as ritual and dialogue, not display.** "The Sculpture's interactive… intention is to be experienced by everyone involved around them." Art is "a two-way street that encourages viewer participation" (Krüg profile). The goal is to "change the way people view their perception of self-awareness, what art is & what it can be."

**Community-built workshops as the engine.** From 2000 to today he has run sculptural build-workshops in San Francisco, Seattle, Austin, Steamboat Springs, Denver, NYC, and Atlanta. Teaching welding and fabrication is part of the art — "forming at times a therapeutic close relationship, to self, and community, which is a life-changing experience."

**Fire as primal human technology.** From *The Communal Caldron of Fire*: fire "nurtured community… a communal place to share stories." He sees modern society as having lost this, and his cauldrons as a way to bring it back — "social enablers" that create a central axis point for people to gather.

**Hybrid wood + LPG fire machines.** Technically, his signature is a hybrid: wood-fired chambers combined with LPG (propane) flame effects, manually operated, with rigorous safety protocols — a form he says he's "been proving through my other art pieces on and off playa since 2011–12."

## Project catalog & timeline

A chronological tour of the major works. (Years reflect his own CV and the source pages; see the honoraria note below for some date discrepancies between sources.)

**HEARTH — 2000.** A giant wood-fired sculpture at "the Heart of Burning Man," designed and built by **Syd Klinge & Charlie Smith** for the 2000 theme "The Body." Conceived around "the seven ages/stages of man." This is where Charlie met Brian Doherty — the relationship that anchors the 2026 project.

**Synapses Tour ("Synapsis") — 2005.** Arguably the purest expression of his "Johnny Appleseed" method. With his partner **Jaime Ladet** (and his children) and a Burning Man grant, he ran **six workshops in six cities** (New York, Atlanta, Austin, Los Angeles, Seattle, San Francisco) over a six-month run, each community welding and designing its own **12–18 ft Fire Cauldron**. All six came together as one installation — a circle of fire cauldrons — at Burning Man 2005, where the groups "met each other" and told their stories through a **symbol system they created themselves** (no words, just the cauldrons' carved imagery). Afterward, each city was **gifted its regional cauldron** to use locally. In the interview Charlie stresses the legacy: "a high percentage of people who took part have accelerated into their own art and their own teams" — it's "still resonating today."

**Fertility caldron series — ~2007.** A set of metal fire-cauldron "effigies of fertility" — documented as *Man, Woman and the Waking Rooster, Mr. Nice Guy, Hot Mama,* and *the Red Hot Cock* — featured at Burning Man in 2007. They functioned as nighttime heat sources and an "entertaining fire TV show," warming participants on their nightly rounds. This is the origin of the recurring **rooster/"cock"** motif that later becomes the 2018 teeter-totter. (Note: *Mr Nice Guy* and *Hot Mama* were still touring as recently as Love Burn 2015.)

**AfrikaBurn / "Triple Bypass" — 2007.** With Jaime Ladet and support from the Black Rock Arts Foundation (and Paul Jorgensen), Charlie led three weekend workshops in **Cape Town, South Africa** to build a community Fire Cauldron for the **first-ever AfrikaBurn** fire festival (July 21–23, 2007). In the interview he calls the project **"Triple Bypass,"** built in-studio over six weeks as part of the **Burning Man global art program** — he frames it as helping *instigate* AfrikaBurn and giving that community "the first Burning-Man-represented artifact" connecting it to the global network.

**The Fleeble Flobbler — 2008.** A 22-ft-tall steel "rotund clown figure" fire cauldron, created for Burning Man via a seed-money grant. **Eight seats** extend from a pivoting basin so participants can ride it — a teetering, eight-seated base "fueled with the fire energy of the earth and powered by the physical energy of the human being." Later repurposed as a flame-effects machine and toured to music/arts festivals. Charlie frames it as "a literal icon of the childlike state of the American ideal."

**24/7 A Time Star ("Timing is Everything") — 2012.** A large-scale interactive **seven-sided star** created for Burning Man 2012, built with **100+ people**. Symbolically packs together the seven days of the week, planetary symbolism, the seven Hindu chakras, the seven levels of heaven, and the seven virtues — "a single tribute to the sun." It teeter-totters (the "push and pull" of the tides). After the playa it had a **2.5-year second life (fall 2015–spring 2018)** installed in Atlanta's **Historic Fourth Ward** activity field via grants from **Art on the BeltLine** and the **Black Rock Arts Organization**, becoming a local icon (it debuted at the 2015 BeltLine Lantern Parade).

**Uncle Charlie's Red Hot Cock / "The Waking Bird Dog" — 2018.** *(See deep dive below.)* The 30-ft flaming teeter-totter — the piece Kris Krüg worked on. A 2018 Burning Man Honorarium project.

**The Solar Scope — 2023 → (in progress).** His stated "what's next" in the 2023 interview: a **mechanical, tuned "time-keeper"** commissioned for a private property near **Knoxville, Tennessee**, attached into the limestone core of a large dome. A homage to **ancient Egyptian and Sumerian gods and the harnessing of time** — and to giant land-art structures / primitive observatories — set on a **60° parallel** to mark equinoxes and solstices, with mechanisms that track planetary arrangements and focus the sun through a central dial. Built with his **cousin (a "math artist")** and young studio interns, combining four machines. Materials are his **"Charlie patchwork"**: copper, brass, glass, stainless steel and carbon steel. *(Status as of 2026 unknown — worth asking.)*

**The Burning Book of Brian — 2026.** *(See dedicated section below.)* The current memorial project.

**Other recurring / named works (mentioned across sources).** A **metal Ganesha / "big metal Buddha"** figure; a **"trail of toys"**; and the **Mr. Nice Guy & Hot Mama** fertility caldrons (still touring as of Love Burn 2015). These round out the menagerie of mythic fire-characters he returns to.

**Ongoing.** Charlie has also produced/co-produced regional burns and shown work at a huge range of festivals (see CV clientele list) — notably co-founding/co-producing **The Love Burn** (Miami; with Glen Grey & Prosperity Angel; official Regional as of 2015) and showing at **Transformus, Burning Flipside, Critical Massive, Alchemy, Euphoria, Afrika Burn,** etc.

## Deep dive: Uncle Charlie's Red Hot Cock (2018)

*The piece Kris built with Charlie.*

- **Also known as:** "The Waking Bird Dog."
- **What it is:** "A large-scale steel and stainless-steel **jackal-headed rooster teeter-totter that burns wood**." Interactive, community-built and -operated mechanical wood-fired cauldrons.
- **Scale:** Footprint **12' × 30' long × 30' tall**. Needs a **50 ft × 40 ft ellipse** on flat, level ground to assemble and operate safely. (Kris remembers it as "30-something feet tall" — the site lists 30' tall.)
- **Status:** **2018 Burning Man Honorarium** project. Also shown at the **Okeechobee Music & Arts Festival, March 2018** (#OMF18) — the year Kris camped with Charlie and the crew (Paperboy, Little Ed) built/ran it; documented in Kris's Flickr album (see `/Red-Hot-Cock-2018`). Charlie's **hand-lettered sign-flag** read *"Uncle Charlie's Cock — Roasted Tube Steaks."*
- **The experience (in his words):** At sunrise, "a radical cacophony of noise rings from beyond… clankity clankity screech rumble rumble… you see the contraption, A giant metallic jackal-headed rooster glowing red hot as it howls fire rocking to and fro across the sunrise, smoke n flame belching from its beak-like jowls and Mohawk-topped head. Someone screams **ROCK THE COCK!** while two large groups of participants — **30 or more total** — start jumping up and down in sequence rocking the gigantic teeter-totter!"
- **Capacity:** Held **2–2.5 cords of firewood** (Kris says two; Charlie says "two and a half"), burning 3–4 times across the week; a **team of ~40** loaded wood (crawling inside the sculpture to stuff it) and ran the burns. Participants donated **meats and sausages**, dropped in through "the cock's ass" / a "poop chute" and tipped out at sunrise as "hundreds of freshly-done meat sticks" to feed the masses.
- **Build cadence:** Kris went out **three weeks early** with Charlie — before Black Rock City's streets were even up — then "shifted into a down gear" during the event to "hold court" at camp and walk visitors out to the sculpture.
- **Scale — sources disagree:** sparceland.com lists **30' tall**; the official Burning Man 2018 guide (quoted in Kris's post) lists **12' wide × 30' long × 25' tall**; Kris's own narrative calls it a **"50-foot tall metal sculpture."** Footprint and the 12'×30' base are consistent across all three; height is the variable. (Kris recalls it as "30-something feet.")
- **Lineage:** Descends from the ~2007 "Red Hot Cock" / waking-rooster fertility series — the motif Charlie has returned to for over a decade.
- **Photo archive — two distinct 2018 events (don't conflate them):**
  - `Red-Hot-Cock-2018/` = **Okeechobee, March 2018** (#OMF18) — Kris's Flickr set (camp, crew, the rooster firing at the festival).
  - `Red-Hot-Cock-BM2018/` = **Burning Man / Black Rock City, Aug 2018** — the honorarium master set: **148 high-res frames** from Kris's Canon 5D Mark IV + Galaxy S9 (3-weeks-early camp setup, night gin-pole raising, the engraved jackal-rooster head on the playa, dust-storm crew moments, the "Johnny Appleseed of Burning Man" banner). The bulk are previously-unpublished. Every frame is visually captioned in `Catalog/images.csv` (`rights=kk-owned`) and exported to `Catalog/manifest.json`.

## Adjacent project: Shrine of Sympathetic Resonance (2018)

*Not Charlie's work, but part of Kris's same Burning Man orbit — documented in his 2023 story alongside the Red Hot Cock, and a useful node in the network.*

- **What:** A sanctuary-pagoda built from **55 grand pianos**, its walls made of **piano harps — ~20,000 musical strings** that echo back every sound. Strings tuned to pentatonic scales based on the **Schumann Resonance** (Earth's fundamental frequency), so anything played sounds musical. Included **five sound caves**. A wooden marvel that **burned to the ground** in classic Burning Man fashion.
- **Lead artist / project lead:** **Tyson Ayers** ([SoundCave.org](https://soundcave.org/); creating since 2006).
- **Co-lead / collaborating artist:** **Leah Desjardins** (also of Waxing Gibbous Apothecary; tea ceremonies; on Sound Cave Projects since 2017).
- **Team:** **Nicholas Christie** (engineer; Dr. Brainlove, Wind Flute), **Shannon Riley** (project manager; founder of **Building 180**), **Brent Bishop** (lighting director, LA), **Charline Formenty** (SF; founder of Carte Blanche), **Mike Brezinski** (social worker & musician).
- **Kris's gallery:** [Flickr album](https://www.flickr.com/photos/kk/albums/72157710406147986); write-up at [kriskrug.co/shrine](https://kriskrug.co/shrine).

## The man himself — from the 2023 videos

*Drawn from Kris's two 2023 videos (full transcripts in `/Transcripts`). This is the human context behind the work — handle with care; the recovery details are sensitive but were shared publicly by Charlie himself.*

**Health, injury & comeback.** Around **2017** Charlie **lost most of the use of his left arm** (he says ~40% function remains) plus back problems — devastating for a self-sufficient sculptor. He **gave his 2017 Burning Man grant back**, asking them to redirect the money to another artist. He was also hit with **terminal misdiagnoses** and "thought I was going to die for a while," sinking into roughly a year and a half of depression. He **rebuilt**, rewrote his proposal, and came back strong for the **2018** honorarium (the Red Hot Cock). Kris notes the contrast plainly: "this is a different man than he was in 2018/2019… I can see you've got more tricks up your sleeve."

**Sobriety & recovery.** Charlie and Kris support each other in **sobriety**. Charlie speaks openly about hitting rock bottom during the pandemic (cocaine; "the cocaine nightmare is real"), checking himself into a program with family support around late 2021, and rebuilding through **personal responsibility** ("I can't blame anybody for my actions"). He participates near-daily in **AA** ("AA light") and uses **SMART Recovery** (smartrecovery.org, a CBT-based program). Recurring theme: "Don't compare your insides to other people's outsides." He now **requires a no-drugs/no-alcohol contract** during his build workshops ("there's a time and a place… we celebrate when we're done").

**Mentorship & the next generation.** Charlie has spent ~17 years balancing his art with raising his **sons** (one, **Casper**, appears in the interview) and pouring into **studio interns** ("a launching pad… if I can show this can be done, it can be done"). His Atlanta scene is a genuine community — crew/artists like **Paperboy** and **Little Ed** build alongside him, and at Burning Man "a steady flow of seemingly every interesting artist on the playa" comes by ASS Camp to "pay respects to Uncle Charlie."

**Range.** Before/around the art career he's been "a janitor, chef, dishwasher, house-builder, broadcast-TV set builder, movie-element fabricator" — "I never stayed in one ditch; I always look at life as a survey: what's next."

**Writing & poetry.** Charlie also writes — his old site hides a "Words" section of 17 typewriter-style poems/song-lyrics (stored as GIF images). They're surreal free-verse list-poems with consumer-culture satire and a recurring **fire / chicken / wolf** thread that feeds straight into his rooster sculptures (*Mr. Nice Guy, Hot Mama, the Red Hot Cock*). "*walking with chickens*" is almost an ars poetica: "a successful failure / one might add?… and yet the fire lives inside / the relentless wolf / poor chickens / cluck." See `/Poetry` for transcriptions and the full catalog.

## The 2026 project: The Burning Book of Brian

**Headline:** A dedicated **memorial fire sculpture** honoring writer **Brian Doherty** — "The Writer," author of *This Is Burning Man* (2004) and a longtime LA Cacophony Society member — who died suddenly on **March 13, 2026**. Charlie and Brian met and bonded at **HEARTH in 2000**, so this closes a 26-year loop. The project got its "juice" at a memorial gathering for Brian at **The Brewery in LA**, where Charlie shared his first hand-drawn sketches.

**The sculpture:**
- A **stylized metallic LPG + wood-fired effigy of a book**, standing **14–15 ft tall**, footprint **12' wide × 8' deep**.
- Sits tilted slightly back, lying open — with a **large human heart piercing the book's core**, suspended in tension.
- An **LPG flame effect pulses from inside the heart** — a literal "thump-THUMP-thump" heartbeat of the playa, the participants, and Brian. Participants can **pump the heartbeat** under the guidance of the FX operator.
- The pages and cover are "skinned" with **dedicated design, text, and symbolism by a collective of 8–10 artists** reflecting their connections to Brian — illuminated by fire and LED at night ("melting pages illuminated by fire").
- Atop the book, left and right, are **stylized ports** for wood load-in and flame flow-out.
- The surrounding art space has benches, an impromptu soapbox, a stylish control box, and a fuel depot, ringed by **6 flagged perimeter poles** with LED festoons inside a **100-ft-diameter activation zone**.

**The burn / ritual:**
- Built as a **community experience** through a series of workshops at **Sparseland Studio in Atlanta**, with Charlie and friends.
- On **Wednesday night** of the event, a **procession** leaves the annual **Cacophony Society Cocktail Hour** (6–7pm, Center Canopy) for a **ONE-TIME-ONLY BURN** — "solemn + joyful," "Brian would like it both ways."
- Framed as a 24/7 space for solitary reflection and festive celebration alike — "weirdos, freaks, art cars, spoken words, performances, music, dance, mostly ridiculousness."

**Fire tech & safety (he's serious about this):**
- A **hybrid machine**: two wood-burn chambers (≈8' tall trapezoid volumes) plus an LPG human-heart flame effect, all manually operated. Directed by Charlie with **2 operators + 8 assistants**.
- Flame effects reach **no more than ~20 ft total height** (heart ports eject 8–10 ft above ground, jetting ~8 ft up).
- **LPG:** fed from 3× 100 lb propane tanks in a ventilated, barriered structure, **≥30 ft** from the ignition area; UL-rated 120 psi regulators; ¼-turn emergency shutoffs; 750 psi-rated hoses; leak-tested before every operation.
- **Suppression:** on-site fire department crew + engine with foam/water; 4× 20 lb ABC dry-chem extinguishers, 2× water extinguishers, 4× 5-gallon water buckets, 2× fire blankets; full procedures manual + MSDS at the control box for the AHJ.
- A detailed **build/setup timeline** (arrival Monday before the event, layout, trench for control/LPG lines, crane/VR for assembly, inspection, test-fire, ready by Sunday) plus a thorough **Leave No Trace (LNT) mooping plan** is documented on his site.

## Brian Doherty — context for the memorial

- **Who:** Brian Doherty, 57, longtime senior editor at *Reason* magazine (joined 1994); author of six books; an LA **Cacophony Society** member and chronicler of fringe/freedom subcultures.
- **Burning Man significance:** Wrote **_This Is Burning Man_ (2004)**, one of the definitive histories of the event. His best-known work overall was *Radicals for Capitalism* (2007).
- **Death:** Died from a fall at **Battery Yates park** along San Francisco Bay in Sausalito; found **Thursday, March 12, 2026** (Charlie's page memorializes the loss as March 13). Covered by *Reason*, the *San Francisco Chronicle*/wire services, and others.
- **Why it matters here:** Brian wasn't just a chronicler — to Charlie and "so many of us," a dear friend. The Burning Book makes the man who wrote *the* book on Burning Man into a burning book.
